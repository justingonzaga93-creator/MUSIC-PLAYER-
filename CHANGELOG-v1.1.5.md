# Quantum Music Player v1.1.5

- Added a Sleep Timer button inside the Playing page, beneath Equalizer.
- Sleep Timer opens a grouped pop-out with Off, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, and 90 minute options.
- Countdown updates live; when it reaches zero, playback pauses.
- Removed the old Sleep Timer control from More/settings to avoid duplicate controls.
- Preserved the existing Equalizer pop-out, navigation, mini-player, and music library.
